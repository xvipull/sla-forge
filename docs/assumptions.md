# Assumptions and limitations

- Data is deterministic, synthetic and contains no customer, employee or production ITSM information.
- The demo measures calendar hours; production SLA clocks need business-hours calendars, holidays and pause rules.
- A ticket with either response or resolution breach is counted as an SLA breach.
- Pending and open tickets use 2025-09-01 as the reporting cut-off.
- Risk scores are transparent triage aids and require operational validation before production use.
