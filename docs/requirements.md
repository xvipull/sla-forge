# Product requirements

## Stakeholders and decisions

| Persona | Decision supported |
| --- | --- |
| IT Service Manager | Prioritize breached or high-risk work and improve SLA attainment. |
| Operations Director | Identify systemic queue bottlenecks and capacity risks. |
| Support Lead | Reduce avoidable transfers, reopens and backlog aging. |

**In scope:** incident ticket events, SLA policy fields, assignment history, queue scorecards and breach-risk triage. **Out of scope:** ITSM writeback, employee performance management and automated ticket closure.

The data owner is Service Operations; refresh is daily. Only synthetic, non-identifying demo data is stored. Acceptance criteria: source and curated ticket counts reconcile, all required controls pass, KPI formulas are documented, and leaders can identify the highest-risk queue and ticket from the dashboard.
